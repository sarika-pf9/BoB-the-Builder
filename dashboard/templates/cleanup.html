Build records

Permanently deletes finished build records (success, failed, or canceled) older than the age below — the database row, its log file, and its qcow2 artifact (if any), all together. This is separate from, and more aggressive than, the automatic background cleanup: that only ever ages out qcow2 and log files on disk and never touches build history. This does, and it cannot be undone. A queued or currently-running build is never affected, no matter how old.
